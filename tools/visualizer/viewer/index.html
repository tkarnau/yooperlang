YOOPERLANG COMPILATION WORLD

A compiler run, laid out as a road through the night. Eight districts, one per stage: source, lex, parse, modules, typecheck, codegen, link, run. Each district is a dark hologram until you walk to its pedestal and press E - that really runs the stage, and the district materializes piece by piece from what the compiler produced.
The bar at the bottom is your debugger: pause a materialization, step it one element at a time, slow it down or speed it up.
Click anywhere to start walking.
